Learn More About Our Affiliated Events

All events will take place at the conference hotel, Renaissance Baltimore Harborplace Hotel (Marriott), in Baltimore, Maryland.

Learn@DLF

Sunday, October 9, 2022

Learn@DLF is our pre-conference workshop day where, through engaging, hands-on sessions, attendees will gain experience with new tools and resources, exchange ideas, and develop and share expertise with fellow community members. The DLF Forum follows Learn@DLF on October 10-12. More details can be found on the Learn@DLF page.

NDSA's Digital Preservation 2022

Wednesday, October 12 – Thursday, October 13, 2022

Digital Preservation 2022: Preserving Legacy is the annual conference of the NDSA, and is expected to be a crucial venue for intellectual exchange, community-building, development of best practices, and national-level agenda-setting in the field. More details can be found on the DigiPres website.

Digitizing Hidden Collections Symposium

Wednesday, October 12 – Thursday, October 13, 2022

In fall 2022, CLIR’s Digitizing Hidden Special Collections and Archives program will host a two-day symposium inviting grant recipients to share lessons from their project work. Recipients’ collective experiences will create opportunities for reflection on the current state and future potential of digitization practice. The event will take place on October 12-13, 2022 directly following the DLF Forum and will be open to the public. More details can be found on the Digitizing Hidden Collection Symposium’s website.
