Meet our 2022 Sponsors

Who and What to Expect at Our Events

We are thrilled to invite you to join us as a sponsor at the 2022 Digital Library Federation (DLF) Forum, Learn@DLF pre-conference, NDSA’s Digital Preservation, and the Digitizing Hidden Collections (DHC) Symposium on October 9-13 in Baltimore, Maryland.

These events are multi-day experiences for learning, networking, and skill-building, working to inspire, connect, and share successes and failures with a dedicated community of digital library practitioners. After two years of meeting online due to the pandemic, we are eager to host a safe, inclusive, in-person event in Baltimore, MD, based on the guiding principle of gratitude.

Sponsoring the DLF Forum connects your brand to a multi-disciplinary cross-sector audience of professionals in the digital library, museum, archives, and cultural heritage fields, from project managers, technologists, and developers to administrators and service providers. The Forum has welcomed professionals from academic, art and cultural heritage, and non-profit organizations, government agencies, and more.

The DLF Forum convenes a community of deeply engaged, hands-on professionals seeking actionable and informative insights to inform decisions for their organizations. With over 20 years of Forum organizing experience, we know what it takes to drive value for our sponsors.

While the Forum is open to all, DLF has a robust membership community, consisting of over 160 organizations. Take advantage of the opportunity to meet this engaged community where they come together across these simultaneous events.

DLF Forum with Learn@DLF Pre-Conference – October 9-12
Annual In-Person Attendance: over 600
The Forum kicks off on October 9 with the pre-conference Learn@DLF featuring hands-on workshops for attendees to learn new tools and exchange ideas with fellow community members. The Forum will follow on October 10-12 where professionals from academic, art, cultural heritage, non-profit organizations, and government agencies from around the country and abroad connect, collaborate, and share their work and experiences.

NDSA’s Digital Preservation – October 12-13
Annual In-Person Attendance: 200
The Digital Preservation conference is the meeting place for NDSA’s 260+ partnering organizations and is a venue for intellectual exchange, community-building, development of good practices, and national-level agenda-setting in the digital stewardship field.

Digitizing Hidden Collections Symposium – October 12-13
Anticipated Attendance: 300
This symposium will invite grant recipients of CLIR’s Digitizing Hidden Special Collections and Archives grant program to build on their collective experiences and reflect on how the digital cultural record can better reflect the diversity of human thought and experience, how law and ethics affect strategies for access, and how technologies and standards can improve discovery and learning.

Check out our sponsorship levels and please don’t hesitate to contact us at forum@diglib.org if you have any questions. Thank you for your consideration in supporting these exciting events!

Sponsorship Levels and Included Benefits

We have a variety of sponsorship opportunities to engage with attendees of the DLF Forum and affiliated events. Check out our tiered levels, add-ons, and unique opportunities. We are always happy to discuss custom sponsorship packages as well so please reach out to forum@diglib.org.

Please note that exhibitor tables require Silver level sponsorship or higher. Exhibitors engage with attendees to make connections and have meaningful conversations. Speak directly to decision makers and other professionals in the industry. Limited space is available, so reserve your spot soon!

Have you sponsored previously? If yes, we’re excited to offer you a 25% discount on any sponsorship level. New sponsors that are a non-profit, educational, or organizations with fewer than 10 full time employees also receive a 25% discount on any sponsorship level.
