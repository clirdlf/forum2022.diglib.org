Gayle Schechter

Gayle (she/her) is DLF’s Program Associate where she manages and provides administrative support to DLF’s working groups and coordinates community outreach (including building the website you’re currently reading). Gayle holds a B.S. in American history and information systems and an M.S. in library and information science with a concentration in archives management. Prior to joining Team DLF in September 2019, Gayle was the digital exhibitions coordinator at an HBCU consortium library. Based in Atlanta, GA, Gayle lives with her chihuahua mix, Rico, who can often be found joining her for working group calls. Find her on Twitter (at)akaGladys.
